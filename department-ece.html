PRACTICAL LEARNING

State-of-the-Art Laboratories

  • VLSI CAD Lab: Cadence Virtuoso, Synopsys, Xilinx Vivado FPGA boards.
  • Embedded Systems & IoT Lab: ARM Cortex, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, sensor kits.
  • Microwave & Optical Comm Lab: Klystron test benches, optical fiber splicing.
  • DSP & Simulation Lab: MATLAB, Simulink, Texas Instruments DSP starter kits.
  • Electronic Devices & Circuits Lab: Digital storage oscilloscopes, signal generators.
