ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Education with purpose and excellence.

G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College is an engineering institution committed to quality technical education, innovation, research and holistic student development.

The college combines strong academic foundations with practical learning, industry exposure, technical activities and a vibrant campus environment.

OUR VISION

To inspire excellence.

To emerge as a centre of excellence in technical education, research and innovation, producing competent professionals and responsible citizens.

OUR MISSION

What we strive to do

  • Provide quality technical education with strong fundamentals.
  • Encourage research, innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Develop industry-ready technical and professional skills.
  • Promote ethics, leadership and social responsibility.
  • Support holistic development in a collaborative environment.
LEADERSHIP

Guided by experience and vision.

Strong academic leadership, experienced faculty and committed administration work together to create an environment where students can learn, innovate and grow.
