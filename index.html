Welcome to Our Institution

Established with the objective of imparting quality technical education, the college is a pioneer in shaping the careers of young minds. Recognized by AICTE and permanently affiliated to JNTUA, we offer NBA-accredited undergraduate programs in various engineering disciplines.

Our autonomous status allows us to design industry-oriented curriculum, ensuring our students are ready for the global workforce.
