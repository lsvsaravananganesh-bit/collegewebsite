Bridging Campus to Corporate Success

The Training & Placement Club (TPC) works alongside the central placement directorate to support pre-final and final year students. TPC organizes daily aptitude speed tests, alumni technical mentorship, resume roasting sessions, and company-specific test rehearsals.
