ABOUT MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Transforming Designs into Motion

The Department of Mechanical Engineering combines fundamental engineering mechanics and thermodynamics with contemporary automation technologies including CAD/CAM modeling, CNC programming, robotics, automotive engineering, and mechatronics.

PRACTICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Workshops & Advanced Laboratories

  • CAD/CAM Prototyping Center: SolidWorks, CATIA, ANSYS, 3D printers.
  • CNC & Machine Tools Lab: CNC turning centers, CNC vertical milling machines.
  • Thermal Engineering Lab: Four-stroke engine test rigs, heat exchanger setups.
  • Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machinery Lab: Pelton wheel, Francis turbines.
  • Mechatronics & Robotics Lab: 6-Axis robotic arms, pneumatic/hydraulic trainers.
