PRACTICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Advanced Civil Laboratories

  • Strength of Materials Lab: 100-Ton Universal Testing Machine (UTM), torsion testers.
  • Surveying & GIS Lab: Electronic Total Stations, Handheld DGPS, ArcGIS software.
  • Concrete & Highway Materials Lab: Compression testing machines, bitumen ductilometer.
  • Geotechnical Engineering Lab: Triaxial shear apparatus, direct shear setups.
  • Computer Aided Design (CAD) Lab: AutoCAD Civil 3D, STAAD.Pro, Revit Architecture.
