ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Powering Sustainable Progress

The Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE) combines core electrical engineering principles with modern digital electronics, smart grid sensors, power converters, programmable logic controllers (PLC), and renewable solar/wind generation systems.

LABORATORIES & EQUIPMENT

Practical Electrical Laboratories

  • Electrical Machines Lab: Transformers, synchronous alternators, DC motors.
  • Power Electronics & Drives Lab: Thyristor converters, PWM inverters, chopper circuits.
  • Power Systems Simulation Lab: MiPower, MATLAB Simulink, ETAP software.
  • Microcontrollers & PLC Automation Lab: Siemens S7 PLCs, SCADA demonstration rigs.
  • Renewable Energy Research Center: Rooftop solar PV testing bench.
